About Ellis & Associates

We've Spent Decades in the Field.
Now We Work for You.

Ellis & Associates is a Michigan-based strategic advisory firm built on something most consulting firms can't claim: real-world experience inside the agencies, missions, and systems we now help clients navigate. With over 75 years of combined experience across law enforcement, corrections, emergency management, and technology integration, our team understands how government agencies fund priorities, run procurement, and make adoption decisions. We don't over-promise. We tell you what's possible, map the path to get there, and do the work.

What We Do

Our Expertise.

01

Government Affairs

Legislation shifts. Priorities change. Agency leadership turns over. We monitor it all, tracking legislative developments, engaging key decision-makers, and advocating for your interests alongside trusted lobbying partners at the state and federal level. Because we came from inside these agencies, we understand not just who to talk to, but what they need to hear.

  • Legislative monitoring & policy analysis
  • Agency engagement & stakeholder mapping
  • Procurement alignment & funding strategy
  • Legislative advising for other lobbying firms
  • Cross-jurisdictional strategy & execution
02

Business Development

Breaking into government markets requires more than a good product. It requires knowing how agencies buy, what they prioritize, and who influences those decisions. We help public safety technology companies increase market presence, navigate complex procurement processes, and build the partnerships that drive long-term adoption across government and first responder ecosystems.

  • Public-sector market entry strategy
  • Strategic introductions & alliance development
  • Channel & partnership development
  • Procurement navigation & positioning
  • First responder agency engagement
03

Security Consulting

Risk in public safety extends beyond physical threats into digital systems, operational vulnerabilities, and public trust. Drawing on decades of real-world law enforcement and emergency management experience, we identify vulnerabilities across technology platforms and organizations and deliver proactive strategies that protect agencies and providers before threats become crises.

  • Vulnerability assessment & risk detection
  • Digital & operational security strategy
  • Reputational risk management
  • Crisis preparedness & response planning
  • Compliance & regulatory guidance

Who We Work With

Industries We Serve.

Public Safety Technology

Your product solves a real problem for law enforcement, fire, EMS, corrections, or emergency management. The challenge isn't the technology — it's navigating how agencies buy, what they prioritize, and who controls the budget. We help you get in front of the right people, aligned with the right funding streams, at the right time.

Technology

You have a product that government agencies need, but breaking into the public sector is a different game than the commercial market. Procurement cycles are long, decision-making is layered, and relationships matter as much as specs. We help you position for contracts, secure endorsements, and build the credibility that moves government buyers.

K-12 & Higher Education

You're trying to get safety legislation passed, secure grant funding, or build partnerships with school districts and universities. The path runs through state government, and knowing how to navigate it makes the difference between a proposal that sits in committee and one that becomes law. We help education-focused organizations move from idea to outcome at the state and local level.

Associations & Trade Groups

Your members are counting on you to protect their interests, shape policy, and make sure their voice is heard in Lansing. That requires more than showing up to hearings. We help associations build ongoing legislative relationships, respond to regulatory threats, and drive the policy outcomes that matter most to the industries you represent.

Emerging Technology

Your technology is ahead of the policy curve. Legislators and agency leaders may not yet have a framework for what you do — and that's both the challenge and the opportunity. We help emerging tech companies shape the conversation, influence early policy decisions, and establish credibility before the market is fully defined.

Healthcare & Behavioral Health

You have a solution that could improve outcomes for first responders, corrections officers, or government employees — but the people making procurement and funding decisions don't know you exist yet. We help behavioral health and wellness organizations break into government markets, align with state funding initiatives, and build the credibility needed to become a trusted partner to public safety agencies.
